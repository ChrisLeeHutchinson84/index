Work

I specialize in both front-end web development and graphic design, offering a comprehensive approach that integrates both technical skill and creative design. In web development, I focus on creating responsive, user-friendly websites using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with particular expertise in building dynamic Single Page Applications (SPAs). My process begins with understanding the client's needs, followed by wireframing, prototyping, and then coding the final product with a focus on performance and interactivity. For graphic design, I follow a structured process that starts with concept development, moving through drafts and revisions, and finishing with high-quality visuals that align with the brand’s identity. By combining these processes, I deliver complete digital solutions that seamlessly blend functionality with engaging, custom-designed graphics and layouts.

About

I have extensive experience in both graphic design and web development, allowing me to blend technical expertise with creative vision to deliver visually appealing and highly functional digital solutions. In graphic design, I’m skilled with industry-standard tools and have a strong understanding of layout, color theory, typography, and branding. This background enables me to create logos, promotional materials, and social media content that are both aesthetically pleasing and aligned with each client’s goals, resulting in cohesive and polished designs.

On the web development side, I specialize in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and front-end frameworks, enabling me to build dynamic, responsive websites and web applications. I have experience developing everything from simple landing pages to complex single-page applications (SPAs), managing the entire front-end development process while incorporating animations, interactive elements, and optimized user experiences. My combined skills in graphic design and web development allow me to offer clients seamless solutions that unify functionality and design, ensuring an engaging user experience and clean, maintainable code.
